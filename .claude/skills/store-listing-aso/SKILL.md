# Store listing & ASO

## Google Play limits
- Title ≤30 chars, short description ≤80, full description ≤4000.
- Feature graphic 1024x500. Screenshots: 2–8 per device, 16:9 or 9:16.
- Keywords come from title + short + full description (no keyword field).

## App Store limits
- Name ≤30, subtitle ≤30, keywords field ≤100 chars (comma separated,
  no spaces, don't repeat words from the name).

## Writing the listing
- Title: brand + 1 keyword (e.g. "פסיבו – משחק הכסף שעובד בשבילך").
- First 2 lines of the full description do 80% of the work: the
  promise + who it's for.
- Structure: hook → how you play (3 bullets) → what you learn → modes
  (family / full) → no internet needed / no signup.
- Honest claims: "learn how passive income works", never "get rich".
- Never name or compare to other brands' board games.

## Keywords to research (HE/EN)
משחק כסף, חינוך פיננסי, הכנסה פסיבית, משחק לוח, משחק משפחתי,
financial literacy game, money board game, passive income, investing game.
Check competitor listings with WebSearch; note their ratings and gaps.

## Screenshots that convert
1. The win moment ("עשיתי את זה! הכסף עובד בשבילי") 2. Board + dice 3. Buying an
apartment 4. Income statement with meter 5. Family mode 6. Professions.
Big caption on top (≤5 words), real gameplay underneath.

## Launch (low budget)
- Pre-launch: 20 testers (closed testing is required by Google Play
  for new personal accounts before production — verify current rule).
- Communities: parenting groups, personal-finance groups, teacher groups.
- Short video hooks: "המשכורת נגמרת ב-20 לחודש? תשחקו בזה",
  turn-by-turn speedruns, parent vs. kid challenge.
- Ask for reviews after a win, never after a loss.
- KPIs: store conversion %, D1 ≥35%, D7 ≥12%, rating ≥4.4.
