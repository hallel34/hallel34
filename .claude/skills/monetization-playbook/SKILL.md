# Monetization playbook

## Principles
- Never pay-to-win. Purchases add variety, not victory.
- Ads never interrupt a turn, a card decision or the win moment.
- Kids may play → follow Google Play Families policy (verify current
  text): neutral age screen, only Families-certified ad SDKs, no
  personalized ads for under-13, no ads that trick kids into tapping.

## Ad placements
| Placement | Type | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Interest-free loan | Rewarded | Optional button in loan dialog |
| Re-roll once per game | Rewarded | Offered after a bad card |
| Bonus "opportunity peek" | Rewarded | See next opportunity card |
| Between games | Interstitial | Max 1 per game, never first session |
No banners on the board (hurts design and readability).

## IAP catalog (starting point — confirm with finance)
- Remove ads: ₪14.90 / $3.99 (also removes interstitials only; rewarded
  remain optional with a free alternative).
- Premium pack: ₪19.90 / $4.99 — 4 extra professions, scenarios
  ("student debt", "young couple"), themed card packs.
- Bundle both: ₪29.90 / $6.99.
Store fee: 15% on first $1M/yr (Google & Apple small-business programs —
verify current terms).

## Free forever
Both modes, 6 base professions, all core cards, save, offline.

## B2B — schools and programs
- Classroom license: teacher dashboard is v2+; v1 offer = ad-free
  school build + lesson plan PDF + worksheet.
- Price idea: ₪300–₪600 per school/year; pilots free for 3 schools in
  exchange for feedback and a quote.
- Targets: municipal education departments, financial-education
  nonprofits, youth movements, bank financial-literacy initiatives.
- Hebrew outreach email: problem (kids don't learn money at school) →
  the game → free pilot offer → 15-minute call.

## Metrics
ARPDAU, rewarded opt-in % (target 20–40% of DAU), IAP conversion
(target 1–3%), ad complaints in reviews (target ~0).
